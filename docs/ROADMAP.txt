Adventure Land roadmap — Eadroma (warrior) + Aedroma (priest)
==============================================================

Snapshot this was written against (Mainframe account data, 2026-09-05)
  Eadroma  warrior  level 18   ~46k gold   main map   online
  Aedroma  priest   level 1    ~70 gold    main map   online

The priest is far behind. Until Aedroma is within a few levels of Eadroma,
the party plan is: farm what the priest can survive, not what the warrior
could solo. XP is shared in a party; dragging a level-1 priest through
hard monsters just gets them killed.

Numbers below come from deployed G.classes, G.skills, and G.monsters
(MCP get_game_data). Monster "attack" is the hit, not a level. If a pack
hits harder than the priest can heal, leave.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Third character — locked in
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Farmer team (3 combat)
  1. Eadroma  warrior  — tank, taunt, pull
  2. Aedroma  priest   — heal warrior first, then mage, then attack
  3. Tonerre  mage — ranged damage + Energize (level 20)

Town / economy (4th, later)
  4. Merchant — luck, bank, stand. Not a farmer.

CODE for the mage: code/tonerre-mage.js
After you create them, set the same name in:
  code/tonerre-mage.js         CONFIG.NAME
  code/eadroma-warrior.js      CONFIG.PARTY (second entry)
  code/aedroma-priest.js       CONFIG.MAGE
Then re-paste all three into the in-game editors.

Create the mage after Aedroma can live on bees/crabs. A dead third farmer
does not help.

Why mage (short)
  Energize at 20 feeds priest MP so Curse / Party Heal (400 MP) actually
  work. Magiport and Burst stay manual / later. Ranger is the only other
  reasonable farmer #3 if you hate mana play — it does not feed the priest.

How to read this roadmap
----------------------------------------------------------------------

These are objectives, not a forced checklist. Upgrade/compound/scroll
work can fail and spend gold — do it only when you decide to.

Stay on the same realm. Party + send_cm do not cross servers.

CODE files
  code/eadroma-warrior.js
  code/aedroma-priest.js
When you change farm monster, change CONFIG.MONSTER in the warrior
(and keep the priest on the warrior's target).

Now — levels 1–20 (you are here)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal: get Aedroma to ~15–18 on the same pack Eadroma is already clearing.

Farm (G.monsters)
  goo        HP 100    XP 100    ATK 5     stay here until priest ~8–10
  bee        HP 300    XP 400    ATK 16    first step up
  crab       HP 400    XP 500    ATK 24    beach / crab packs
  snake      HP 720    XP 960    ATK 24    only if priest heals keep up

Do not jump to minimush (ATK 120) or poisio (ATK 240) yet.

Warrior skills you already have (no level gate)
  Charge   +30 speed, 40s CD, 0 MP
  Taunt    40 MP, 3s CD, 200 range — pull off the priest
  Dash     120 MP — skip until you have MP to spare

Priest skills
  Heal         shares the attack cooldown — heal first, then hit
  Curse        400 MP — skip until MP pool / Energize exists
  Party Heal   400 MP — same
  Revive       500 MP + Essence of Life — keep a life essence for later

Gear (cheap, high impact)
  Eadroma: keep Blade equipped (yours was +4 in inventory). Do not
           dump gold into high upgrades yet; +4 to +6 is plenty.
  Aedroma: staff equipped, carry hpot0/mpot0. Warrior should almost
           never drink HP pots if the priest is in range (already in CODE).
  Buy extra MP pots for the priest; heal is MP-hungry as they grow.

Party habits
  Warrior holds aggro. Priest stays ~40–80 px back (heal range 120).
  If the priest has aggro, warrior Taunts.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Farm
  armadillo  HP 1600   XP 1720   ATK 20    comfortable physical pack
  croc       HP 3200   XP 3600   ATK 48    next physical step
  tortoise   HP 7200   XP 5200   ATK 36    slow, good XP, long fights
  osnake     HP 720    XP 1600   ATK 24    if the pack is live

Avoid until priest+warrior are solid
  poisio     ATK 240
  stoneworm  ATK 360
  minimush   ATK 120 ranged

Warrior
  Still Charge / Taunt. Save gold for a better sword/shield rather than
  Cleave (level 52, needs axe or scythe, 720 MP).

Priest
  More INT / MP so Curse can land occasionally. Curse: +20% damage taken,
  -20% damage dealt, -20 speed, 5s, 400 MP. Use on the current tank target
  only, not on every goo.

If the mage exists and is 20+
  Mage Energize -> priest every few seconds. This is when Curse/Party Heal
  become real.

Levels 40–55
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Farm
  croc / tortoise still fine
  spider     HP 18000  XP 12000  ATK 80     party content
  scorpion   HP 24000  XP 20000  ATK 100    party content
  prat       HP 9200   XP 7600   ATK 320    only with reliable heals

Warrior 52
  Cleave   720 MP, axe/scythe, 160 range — AoE when you actually wear an axe
  Stomp    120 MP, basher, 24s CD — stun, different weapon

Do not swap off a good sword just to press Cleave. Weapon type is a build
choice. For a priest-supported tank, sword/shield or spear is often
smoother than axe until MP and armor are high.

Priest 55
  Absorb Sins  200 MP, 240 range — pull aggro off the warrior onto you.
  That is a *save the warrior* button, not a farming loop. Warrior should
  Taunt back.

Merchant (if you created #4)
  Level 40 Mluck: luck buff on farmers. Park merchant in town, buff
  whoever is farming, keep the stand honest (no spam listings).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Levels 55–70
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Warrior 60  Hardshell — physical mitigation, 16s CD, 8s duration, 480 MP
Warrior 68  Agitate — taunt all nearby monsters (420 MP). Dangerous if
            the priest cannot cover the pack.
Warrior 70  War Cry — party damage buff, 60s CD, 8s. Save for bosses.

Priest 64  Phaseout — needs a shadow stone, panic button
Priest 70  Dark Blessing — +25% damage, 60s CD, 900 MP. Boss button.

Mage 60    Reflection on the tank for some fights
Ranger 60  3-shot if you went ranger instead

Farm
  Only move to high-ATK maps (mole 480, wolfie 320, bigbird 480) when
  Hardshell + priest heal + potions cover a mistake. xscorpion / bscorpion
  are endgame packs, not a level-60 tourist stop.

Economy and items (all levels)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not automate upgrades, compounds, NPC dumps, or public trade listings
unless you set exact item keys, max levels, and gold caps yourself.

Useful habits
  Bank gold you do not need to carry (warrior CODE currently does not
  auto-bank — keep that off until you ask for it).
  Keep scrolls (scroll0 / cscroll0) in the bank, not the farm inventory.
  Booster (if you own one): XP while fighting, Luck on bosses, Gold when
  you actually loot a pile of chests — see https://adventure.land/docs

Eadroma already had: blade, hpbelt, ringsj, gem0, potions.
Aedroma started with gift helmet / staff / shoes and 200/200 pots.
