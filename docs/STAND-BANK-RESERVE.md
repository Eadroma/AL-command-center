
Withdrawn stand stock retains its reservation until publication or removal of the listing rule; production delays do not expire it. Bank navigation stays locked through the return to the stand so NPC sales cannot interrupt the exit.

Active stock reservations are held in memory; persistent storage is only a restart backup. Storage failures must not trigger a deposit/withdraw cycle.

Before closing a stand, remember its visible sell listings. Closed-stand snapshots can omit extended trade slots; restocking uses the remembered snapshot and refreshes it when the stand is open.

A matching posted sell listing fulfils the rule: carried surplus at that exact level is bankable, including non-upgrade items. Policy reads do not delete reservation state; verified surplus deposits clear it. Locked and bound items remain protected.
