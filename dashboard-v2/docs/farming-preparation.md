# Farming preparation and coordinated retreat

## Measured advisor

Farming / Targets ranks attributed history by net gold per observed character-hour.
It uses the exact chosen participant set, excluding bosses and unattributed legacy
history. Terrain/server groups remain separate. Rates use matched economy coverage;
unknown prices suppress net. Confidence describes sample duration/coverage, not a
statistical survival guarantee. Staging changes only the fallback target.

## Shared equipment and checklist

Farming / Equipment and Inventory expose one simultaneous-build allocation plan.
Higher minimum levels are resolved first; each observed slot is consumed once.
Owner copies take priority over unlocked/unbound bags elsewhere and the newest
bank snapshot. Inherited equipment remains reserved. Gear worn by another owner
is never offered for handoff. Bank metadata does not expose all modifiers, locks
or owner bindings: bank suggestions require verification before retrieval.
Selection is local/read-only; transfers are planned rather than executed.

Party's departure disclosure and Farming / Loot & safety show fresh runtime,
health, mana, potion presence, bag room, configured consumables, gear availability,
active safety holds and required healer proximity. These are preparation checks,
not deployment gates or full-session supply forecasts. Unknown defaults are not
approved. Solo guard determines the intended solo participant in these views.

## Coordinated retreat

Opt-in in danger settings; safety must also be enabled. Farmers broadcast their
local safety/death state every five seconds through CM. Receiver checks the
configured farmer sender, profile, risk run, realm and payload type. Fresh peer
holds pause new pulls and navigation; each farmer uses its configured hold/town
action. Healing/potions/self-defence continue. Peer holds are not rebroadcast,
avoiding feedback loops. Peer evidence expires after 20 seconds. This cannot
coordinate clients with stopped CODE or disconnected sockets. Merchant excluded.

Save and deploy the updated farmer bundles to activate coordination. No settings
or running characters are changed by simply opening these views.
